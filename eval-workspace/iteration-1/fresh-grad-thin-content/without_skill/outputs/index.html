01 — Final year project

Sign language → text

For my final year project I built an app that watches a signer's hand through a camera and turns the signs into written text. It uses MediaPipe to find the hand, so the app works with landmarks instead of raw pixels — the same 21 points you see moving above.

step 1

Camera

A webcam frame of the signer's hand.

step 2

MediaPipe

Detects the hand and returns 21 landmark points.

step 3

Recognise

The hand's landmark positions are turned into a sign.

step 4

Text

Recognised signs appear as text on screen.
